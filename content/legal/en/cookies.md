## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. "Similar technologies" are other ways of storing or reading information on your device (for example local storage) or of sending data from your browser to a third party (for example when a map image is loaded). The ePrivacy rules require your consent for all of them, except those that are strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for.

## What we use

We keep this list short on purpose. Strictly necessary items are always on; everything else is only used if you accept it, and you can change your mind at any time.

## Your choice

When you first visit {{brand}} we ask for your choice. "Accept all" and "Reject all" are equally easy, and nothing optional runs before you decide. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as "Reject all".

Your choice is stored for 12 months, after which we ask again. We also ask again if this policy changes materially. Each time you make a choice we keep a record of it (a random id, the choice, the date and the version of this policy) so that we can prove it, as the GDPR requires.

## Browser settings

You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, you will not be able to sign in.
