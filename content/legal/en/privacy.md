## 4. Our legitimate interests

Where we rely on legitimate interest, it is to keep {{brand}} safe and trustworthy (preventing fraud, spam and the harvesting of phone numbers), to keep records of our moderation decisions and to give owners simple statistics about their listings. We have weighed these interests against your rights: we use hashed IP addresses instead of plain ones, keep the data only for short periods and never use it for advertising. You can object at any time (see section 9).

## 5. Automated checks and moderation

To fight spam and scams, some checks are automated: messages containing links and listing texts containing phone numbers, emails or links are refused, and the number of actions per hour is limited. The first listing of every new owner is reviewed by a person before it is published. We do not take decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects on you or similarly significantly affect you (Art. 22 GDPR). When we reject or remove a listing we tell the owner why, and the owner can ask us to review the decision.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access** your data and receive a copy of it;
- **rectify** inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **erase** your data ("right to be forgotten");
- **restrict** the processing of your data in certain cases;
- **data portability**: receive the data you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format;
- **object** at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests;
- **withdraw your consent** at any time, as easily as you gave it, without affecting what we did before;
- **lodge a complaint** with a data protection authority.

The quickest way: in your account, **Settings** lets you correct your profile, **download all your data** (JSON) and **delete your account** with all its listings, photos and messages, immediately. For anything else use our [privacy request form](/privacy/request) or write to [{{privacyEmail}}](mailto:{{privacyEmail}}).

We answer within one month. That period can be extended by two further months for complex requests; if so we will tell you why within the first month. Exercising your rights is free. If we have reasonable doubts about your identity we may ask you to confirm it, using only what is necessary.

## 10. Minors

{{brand}} is intended for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or publish a listing. If we learn that a minor has given us personal data, we will delete it.

## 11. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (HTTPS), passwords stored only as secure hashes, strict access rules at database level so that each user can only read their own private data, the removal of location metadata from photos, hashed IP addresses, protection against automated abuse and regular reviews. Only people who need it for their work can access personal data. If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the competent authority within 72 hours and, where required, inform you without undue delay.

## 12. When you publish

When you publish a listing you decide what to show. Please do not include personal data of other people (for example tenants in photos, documents or car number plates). If you share your address or other details with someone outside {{brand}}, that person becomes responsible for how they use them.

## 13. Changes

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. We will publish the new version here with its date and, for important changes, inform signed-in users and ask again for cookie consent when needed.
